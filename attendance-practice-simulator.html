Sync vs. Async: How Many QR Codes?
Some training days combine both session types — know when to scan once, and when to scan twice.
● Sync session

Live, instructor-led class

Whether in-person or over Zoom, scan the attendance QR once at the start of the live session to mark yourself present.

📷 1 QR scan
● Async session

Self-paced online module

Complete the e-learning material in your own time, then scan a separate QR code to log your async attendance.

📷 1 QR scan
📅 On days with both a sync and an async session, you'll need to complete this walkthrough twice — once for each QR code. Skipping one means that portion of attendance won't be recorded.
